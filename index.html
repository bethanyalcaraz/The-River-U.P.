Our Vision
“We envision a gospel-centered church in Rapid River where people find Jesus, find real community, grow as lifelong followers of Jesus, and help others find Him too.”

The River will be an authentic community of believers that is passionate about Christ and the gospel saturating the Upper Peninsula. We believe that the gospel is for everyone. A church isn’t a building — it’s a group of people who are committed to God and serving the world around them for the sake of the gospel. We want to see the power of God displayed through changed lives.

Does that sound intriguing? Consider joining us!
